Common Questions

  • Restore purchases — Open the app → Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases. On the paywall, use the Restore Purchases link at the bottom.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription — Subscriptions are managed by Apple: device Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or the App Store app → profile → Subscriptions.
  • Delete your account — Open the app → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This clears your server-side session, entitlement cache, and any server-side records linked to your anonymous identifier (see the Privacy Policy). Your Apple ID and any active subscription are managed by you under your device's Apple ID settings.
  • Withdraw AI data-processing consent — Open the app → Settings → AI Data Processing Consent, and toggle it off.
  • Data deletion requests — See Section 9 of the Privacy Policy, or email us.
  • In crisis? — If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services right away, and see findahelpline.com for free, confidential support in your country.
