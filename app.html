A daily rhythm

  • Daily verse, updated every day — today's verse plus related verses for context (English WEB + Chinese CUV). Free, forever.
  • Write down what's on your mind — AI locates a relevant Bible passage and generates a complete devotional for your situation.
  • One devotional, five parts — your question · the located scripture · a plain-words insight · one action step · a sincere prayer.
  • Home-screen widget & daily reminder — today's verse at a glance.
  • Bookmarks & history follow you — saved to your account (Sign in with Apple, anonymous) and restored on any device.

Built to be trusted

Privacy-first by design. No ads, no analytics SDKs, no trackers. Sign-in is optional and uses an anonymous identifier — never your email or name. AI input is processed in the EU and kept only in your deletable devotional history. See the Privacy Policy.

AI never fabricates scripture. Verse text is always looked up from the built-in public-domain Bible (CUV 1919 + WEB, 31,091 verses) — never recalled from AI memory.

Crisis-safe. If you share thoughts of self-harm, the app responds with vetted help resources instead of generated content.

每天的节奏

  • 每日经文，天天更新——今日经文 + 相关经文(英文 WEB + 中文和合本)；永久免费。
  • 写下近况——AI 从圣经定位相关经文，为你生成一份完整的灵修。
  • 一份灵修，五个部分——你的提问 · 定位的经文 · 通俗解读 · 一条行动建议 · 一段真诚的祷告。
  • 桌面小组件 + 每日提醒——今日经文一目了然。
  • 收藏与历史跨设备跟随——保存在你的账号下(Apple 登录，匿名标识)，换机即恢复。

值得托付

隐私优先。无广告、无分析 SDK、无追踪。登录可选,且只使用匿名标识——绝不需要你的邮箱或姓名。AI 输入在欧盟处理;仅保存于你账号下可随时删除的灵修历史。详见隐私政策。

AI 不伪造经文。经文正文永远由内置的公有领域圣经(和合本 1919 + 英文 WEB,31,091 节)按出处取出,绝不靠 AI 记忆复述。

危机安全。若你写下自伤的念头,App 会回应经过审核的帮助资源,而不是生成内容。
