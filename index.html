DailyDevotion AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

1. Introduction

DailyDevotion AI ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect and Process

We design the app to handle as little data as possible. You can read, bookmark, and use the widget without any account; only the optional AI devotional feature uses Sign in with Apple to verify your identity (see below). We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contact details. Specifically:

  • No account required to read — You do not need an account to read, bookmark, or use the widget. The optional AI devotional feature uses Sign in with Apple; we request no email or name (no scopes), so Apple sends our server only a stable, anonymous user identifier.
  • No personal data collection — We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information.
  • No usage analytics — We do not use any analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.).
  • No advertising or hardware identifiers — We do not collect the IDFA, location data, or device hardware IDs. The RevenueCat SDK may access the IDFV (a per-vendor device identifier) solely for subscription entitlement verification; we treat it as a non-identifying device identifier and do not use it for tracking. (A random, anonymous install identifier is also generated for entitlement and quota purposes — see "Operational & Security Data" below.)
  • No religious or other special-category profiling. The App provides Christian devotional content, but we do not ask you to state your religion, and we do not assign, store, or process any religious-belief label, profile, or category about you. The fact that you use this App is not treated by us as information about your religious beliefs. If you voluntarily mention faith-related matters in your text input for the AI devotional, that input is processed solely to generate your devotional (see Section 4) and is never used to profile or categorize you.

Exception — AI Devotional Feature: When you use the AI devotional feature, your text input is temporarily transmitted to our server and processed by a third-party AI provider to generate your devotional. This data is not stored on our servers after the response is generated (it may be cached on your own device as part of your recent devotional history — see Section 3). See Section 4 for details.

Operational & Security Data: To verify subscriptions and entitlements and to prevent abuse, our server temporarily records an anonymous, randomly generated app identifier and the requesting IP address. These are used solely for entitlement verification, rate limiting, and abuse prevention — they are not linked to your name, email, or any other identity, and are not used for any other purpose. They are kept only in the server's volatile memory for as long as needed for these checks and are cleared whenever the server process restarts. Request IP addresses may also appear in standard operational and security logs retained by our hosting provider (Render) for its standard log retention period — currently about 7 days on our (free) plan, and up to about 30 days if the plan is upgraded — to operate and protect the service; this data is never written to a permanent application database.

Install identifier stored on your device: The app also generates a random, anonymous install identifier (not tied to your identity) and stores it in your device's secure keychain, so that your subscription and daily AI quota are preserved if you reinstall the app. This identifier stays on your device under your control; it is not used for tracking and is not shared with advertisers. Clearing the app's data from within the app does not erase this keychain identifier (it is retained deliberately to prevent quota abuse); deleting and reinstalling the app, or resetting your device, removes it.

Sign in with Apple (AI feature, optional): To use the AI devotional feature, the app offers Sign in with Apple. We request no scopes, so Apple provides our server only a stable, anonymous user identifier (never your email or name). Our server verifies Apple's cryptographic signature on this identifier and issues its own short-lived session; it shares the anonymous identifier with RevenueCat so your subscription entitlement is bound to your verified identity and cannot be impersonated. You can use the app fully without signing in except for the AI feature, and you can delete your account at any time in Settings (this deletes your RevenueCat subscriber record and our cached session data; the Apple ID credential itself is managed by you under your device's Apple ID settings).

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers:

  • Your language preference (English/Chinese)
  • Your dark mode preference
  • Your bookmarked Bible verses
  • Your recent AI devotional history, including the situation text you entered (cached on your device so you can revisit past devotionals; never sent back to our servers)

You can clear this data — including your recent devotional history — at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by deleting the app.

iCloud sync (optional): If you have iCloud enabled, the on-device data listed above (language/theme preferences, bookmarks, recent devotional history, and your AI-consent setting) may sync across your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID via Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage. This sync happens within your own Apple account — the data is still never sent to our servers — and is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can disable it from your device's iCloud settings.

4. AI-Generated Devotional Content

When you use the AI devotional feature, the following occurs:

  • Your text input (describing your situation) is sent to our server to generate a personalized devotional.
  • We process this input solely for generating the devotional response.
  • We do not log, store, or retain your input text on our servers after the response is generated. Your input is cached only on your own device as part of your recent devotional history (see Section 3) — never on our servers.
  • AI-generated content is for encouragement only, may be inaccurate or reflect biases, and does not constitute professional counseling, medical or mental-health advice, or religious authority. Verify important matters against Scripture and consult qualified professionals as needed.

Legal basis for processing (European users): Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your text input for AI devotionals based on Article 9(2)(a) — your explicit consent, given when you read the consent disclosure and start using the app (or, if you skip past it, the first time you tap "Generate"). You may withdraw this consent at any time — see "Withdrawing AI consent" in Section 9. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Automated decision-making: The AI devotional is generated entirely by automated means without human review. Under GDPR Article 22, you have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing where it produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you. The devotional content is presented as encouragement only — it does not constitute professional advice (medical, mental-health, legal, or financial) and is not used to make any decision about you as an individual. If you object to receiving AI-generated content without human oversight, please do not use this feature.

Data controller and processor: For purposes of applicable data-protection law, we (the app developer) are the data controller — we decide what data is processed and why. Mistral AI (a French/European company) is the data processor, acting on our instructions to generate the devotional content. Your relationship with Mistral is governed by Mistral's own privacy policy and terms.

Where your input is processed: Our backend servers are located in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). When you use the AI devotional feature, your text input is transmitted to these EU-based servers, and our AI provider (Mistral) also processes it in the European Union (EU data residency is Mistral's default). After your devotional is generated, the input is not stored on our servers (see Section 4).

Data residency and transfers: Your input is processed and stored within the European Union — both our backend (Frankfurt, Germany) and our AI provider (Mistral) operate in the EU. Should any processing ever need to occur outside the EU in the future, we would rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (or an equivalent lawful safeguard). We do not transfer your data to China, or to any jurisdiction lacking an adequate level of data protection.

We rely on Mistral's EU data-residency commitments and data-processing terms to protect your input during processing.
    • No training on your input: We use Mistral AI's paid API. Under Mistral's Commercial Terms (Section 4.2), Mistral does not use Customer Data (your input) or Outputs to train its models.

RevenueCat receives a randomly generated, anonymous app identifier and purchase metadata — no name, email, or contact information. This is not linked to your identity. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not integrate any advertising networks, social media SDKs, or tracking tools.
