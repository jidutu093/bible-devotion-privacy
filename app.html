A daily rhythm

  • Daily verse — A carefully selected Bible passage each day (English WEB + Chinese CUV), with related verses for context. Free, forever.
  • AI devotional — Describe what you're going through in your own words; receive scripture-based reflection, practical steps, and a short prayer. Available in English and Chinese.
  • Bookmarks & history — Save verses, revisit your recent devotionals, and keep a daily streak.
  • Home screen widget & daily reminder — Today's verse at a glance.
  • Syncs across your devices — Bookmarks and settings via your own iCloud. Optional.

Privacy-first by design. No ads, no analytics SDKs, no trackers. Sign-in (Apple only, optional) uses an anonymous identifier — never your email or name. Your input is processed in the EU and never stored on our servers. See the Privacy Policy.

Crisis-safe. If you share thoughts of self-harm, the app responds with vetted help resources instead of generated content.
