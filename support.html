Common Questions

Subscriptions & billing

  • Will I be charged during the trial? No. The 7-day trial is completely free. You're only charged to your Apple ID if you don't cancel before the trial ends.
  • How am I billed? Your subscription auto-renews at the selected plan unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • How do I cancel? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: device Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or the App Store app → profile → Subscriptions. Cancel anytime — it takes effect from the next period.
  • Does it work on a new device? Yes. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID — open the app on the new device and tap "Restore Purchases" (app Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases, or the Restore link at the bottom of the paywall).
  • Can I keep using it without subscribing? Yes. The daily verse, home widget, and bookmarks are free forever, no subscription needed.

App & privacy

  • Can the AI make up or distort scripture? No. The AI only picks the verse reference and writes the reflection and prayer; the verse text itself is always looked up from the bundled public-domain Bible (Chinese Union Version 1919 + World English Bible, 31,091 verses), so scripture cannot be fabricated. Devotionals are for encouragement only, not authoritative teaching.
  • What happens to what I write? Your input is used solely to generate that devotional — never for AI training. It lives only in your in-app devotional history and can be deleted anytime.
  • What if I hit the 30/day limit? Generation pauses for the rest of the day and resumes the next day. 30/day is far beyond typical use.
  • Revoke AI data-use consent — Open the app → Settings → Data & Privacy → AI Data Use, then tap "Revoke Consent".
  • Delete Account — Open the app → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This clears your server-side session, entitlement cache, and any server-side records linked to your anonymous identifier (see the Privacy Policy). Your Apple ID and any active subscription are managed by you under your device's Apple ID settings.
  • Data deletion requests — See Section 9 of the Privacy Policy, or email us.

In crisis? If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services right away, and see findahelpline.com for free, confidential support in your country.

常见问题

订阅与扣费

  • 试用期内会扣费吗？ 不会。7 天试用完全免费，且仅在试用结束前未取消时，才从你的 Apple ID 扣除订阅费用。
  • 订阅如何扣费？ 订阅按所选方案从你的 Apple ID 自动续费，除非在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消。
  • 如何取消？ 订阅由 Apple 管理：设备「设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅」，或 App Store → 头像 → 订阅。随时取消，下一周期起生效。
  • 换设备还能用吗？ 可以。订阅绑定你的 Apple ID，在新设备打开 App，点「恢复购买」（App 设置 → 订阅 → 恢复购买，或订阅页底部的「恢复购买」链接）即可。
  • 不订阅还能用吗？ 可以。每日经文、桌面小组件与经文收藏永久免费，无需订阅。

App 与隐私

  • AI 会不会编造或曲解经文？ 不会。AI 只选出经文出处并撰写解读与祷告；经文正文永远由内置的公有领域圣经（和合本 1919 + 英文 WEB，31,091 节）按出处取出，AI 无法伪造经文。灵修内容仅供鼓励参考，不构成权威解经。
  • 我写下的心事会被如何处理？ 你的输入仅用于为你生成当次灵修，不用于 AI 训练；只保存在你 App 内的灵修历史中，可随时删除。
  • 每天最多 30 次，用完了怎么办？ 当天到达上限后暂停生成，次日自动恢复。30 次/天远超日常所需。
  • 撤回 AI 数据使用同意：打开 App → 设置 → 数据与隐私 → AI 数据使用，点「撤回同意」。
  • 注销账号：打开 App → 设置 → 账号 → 注销账号。这会清除服务端的会话、权益缓存及与您的匿名标识关联的服务端记录（详见隐私政策）。Apple ID 凭据及任何有效订阅由您在设备的 Apple ID 设置中自行管理。
  • 数据删除请求：见隐私政策第 9 节，或邮件联系我们。
